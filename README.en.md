# Quillo for Mac

[日本語](README.md) | **English**

**Turn your speech into captions, and your slides into short translated summaries.**

Quillo is a Mac app that displays floating panels over your usual presentation tools, including PowerPoint, Keynote, and Canva.

## Why I made it

PowerPoint's translated captions are useful, but I ran into two problems in my teaching setup:

- With a windowed slideshow on my Mac, I couldn't see my presenter notes, making it difficult to check **what I wanted to say next**.
- I wanted students to understand **the content on the slides**, as well as what I was saying.

I made Quillo to handle captions separately from the presentation app and provide short translated summaries of slide content. Slides and presenter notes stay in your usual presentation app.

## What it does

- **Live captions and translation** — Speak Japanese or English; the original text and translation update as you speak.
- **Slide summaries** — Select an area to read and show translated key points in a separate panel. Move or resize selections later; multiple areas have separately numbered summaries.
- **Flexible placement** — Drag the panels anywhere and adjust their size, fonts, and backgrounds.
- **Display choices** — Show the original, the translation, or both. Supports Japanese, English, Simplified Chinese, and Traditional Chinese.
- **Choose caption history** — Show one or two earlier sentences dimmed, or turn history off in settings. Font sizes adjust automatically for both originals and translations. No scrolling.
- **Class glossaries** — Add terminology for each set of teaching materials to help recognition and keep translations consistent.

Microphone input supports **Japanese and English**. Caption translation targets, material languages, and typed input support **Japanese, English, Simplified Chinese, and Traditional Chinese**. Choose the spoken and material languages separately.

## Getting started

**This is a source-code prototype.** A ready-to-install, notarized app is not available yet. See the [setup guide (Japanese)](docs/SETUP.md) for build instructions.

- **Mac:** Apple Silicon (M1 or later), macOS 15 or later.
- **Captions:** Microphone and speech recognition permissions, speech data for the spoken language, and translation data. Microphone input for a language is unavailable until its speech data finishes downloading.
- **Summaries:** Ollama with the local model `qwen3.5:9b`. Caption translation does not require Ollama.

The controls are currently in Japanese:

1. Under 「言語の設定」, choose 「話す言語」 (spoken language), 「資料の言語」 (material language), and 「学生に見せる言語」 (student display language). Captions and summaries share the student display language.
2. Click 「話して字幕を出す」 to start microphone captions.
3. For summaries, click 「資料の画面を選ぶ」 and select the slide area.
4. Open 「設定」 to adjust appearance and glossaries.

**Share or project the entire screen, including the caption and summary panels.** Sharing only the presentation window will not include the separate panels.

## Things to know

- Speech recognition, translation, and summaries run on your Mac. Initial language and model downloads require internet access.
- Captions show the current sentence, optionally with one or two earlier sentences. Exceptionally long text in a small panel shows its tail; the transcript keeps the full text. Each selected area gets a summary sized to fit the panel.
- Recognition, translation, and AI summaries can contain errors or take time. Test long sessions and full-screen presentations in your own teaching setup.
- Quillo does not save audio or video recordings. Caption logs are cleared when the app quits unless you explicitly save them.
